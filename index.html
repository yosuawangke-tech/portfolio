About Me

A teacher who thinks
like a designer.

My name is Josua Wangke. I live at the intersection of education and creativity — where the most powerful lesson isn't the most detailed one, but the one that's simply impossible to forget.

As a junior high school teacher in North Sulawesi, I've learned what holds student attention and what loses it. That insight shapes every creative project I take on — whether it's a branded video, a photo series, or educational content.

I'm a creative professional in growth mode: hungry to learn, disciplined in craft, and completely committed to quality at every frame.

My Process

How I think
before I create.

01

Understand

I start by listening deeply — the goal, the audience, and what success truly looks like. No assumptions, only clarity.

02

Conceptualize

I map the visual direction: structure, hierarchy, emotional tone. Great output always begins with intentional planning.

03

Create

Precision execution — editing, compositing, color grading, sequencing. I hold myself to the highest quality at every frame.

04

Refine & Deliver

I review, tighten, and polish until it feels exactly right. Then deliver with context so you understand every choice made.
