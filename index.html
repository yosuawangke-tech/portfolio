About Me

A teacher who thinks
like a designer.

My name is Josua Wangke. I live at the intersection of education and creativity — a place where the most powerful lesson isn't the most detailed one, but the one that's simply impossible to forget.

As a junior high school teacher in North Sulawesi, I've learned what holds student attention and what loses it. That insight shapes every creative project I take on — whether it's a branded video, a photo series, or an educational content piece.

I'm a creative professional in growth mode: hungry to learn, disciplined in craft, and completely committed to quality at every frame.

My Process

How I think
before I create.

01

Understand

I start by listening deeply — what's the goal, who's the audience, and what does success look like? No assumptions.

02

Conceptualize

I sketch the direction: structure, visual hierarchy, and emotional tone. Great output starts with intentional planning.

03

Create

Execution with precision — editing, compositing, color grading, sequencing. Quality at every single frame.

04

Refine & Deliver

I review, tighten, and polish until it feels right. Then I deliver with context — so you understand what you have.
